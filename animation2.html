Why OpenShell

Built for watching,

not for trusting blindly. Every layer of the agent's work has a seat in the audience.

◐

True live diffs

fs.watch streams every workspace change into the editor, diffed against the baseline captured when the session started — while the agent is still mid-sentence.

〰

Streaming timelines

Reasoning, tool calls, and text arrive paced exactly as they stream — collapsible thinking, task cards, and a working summary derived live from the session phase.

⧉

Concurrent sessions

Open another workspace and a matching panel appears — its own tree, diffs, usage, and terminal. Editor focus follows whichever stage you're watching.

⇥

Queue & steer

Prompts queue per session. Follow-ups steer a running agent instead of waiting for it — the message queue persists across restarts.

▮

Real terminals

A node-pty tray per workspace. Run the commands the agent suggests right under the diff and watch the tree light up in response.

◍

Private by design

Everything runs on your machine against your own opencode2 service and credentials. No cloud middleman, no telemetry — your repo, your agent.
